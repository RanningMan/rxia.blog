Most advice on finding users assumes one thing: your buyers post in English, and you reply in English. If you sell to the world, that leaves most of the conversation out.

Every day, people ask for exactly what you built, on Reddit, Hacker News, X, Bluesky and LinkedIn, in Japanese, Spanish, German, Korean and Portuguese. "Is there an app that...", "What do you use for...", "I'm switching away from...". Those posts are the warmest leads on the internet, and most founders never see them because they only search in one language.

The gap runs the other way too. If you're a founder in Seoul, Shanghai or Tokyo, you can read the English threads, but writing a natural reply in a second language is slow and tiring. So the posts sit there, unanswered.

I built [LeadFound](/to-find-users-for-vinciphoto-i-built-leadfound/) for both sides of that gap. It finds high-intent posts in any language, scores how ready the person is to try something, shows you the post in your own language, drafts a reply in your language, and translates it back into theirs. You copy it and post it yourself, under your own name. Nothing is ever posted for you.

## What selling in the global market means for a small team

- Search in the languages your buyers use, not only the one you think in.
- Read and write in the language you're fastest in.
- Reply where the question was asked, in the language it was asked in.
